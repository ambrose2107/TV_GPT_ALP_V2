Quick guide: profit-taking and buyback

Use the watchlist as a review queue, not an instruction to sell. A large unrealized gain alone is not a pullback signal. Consider partial profit-taking only when the position is oversized or multiple independent signals weaken; define the re-entry trigger before selling.

  1. Check extension: distance above the 20-day mean and recent return.
  2. Check trend damage: price below the 20-day mean and 20-day mean below the 50-day mean.
  3. Plan: record the trim size, invalidation level, and buyback condition (e.g. reclaim of a moving average or stabilization near a defined support zone).
  4. Account for friction: taxes, spread, slippage, overnight gaps and the possibility that the stock never returns to the intended dip.
  5. Avoid all-or-nothing timing: a staged trim and staged re-entry can reduce dependence on picking exact tops/bottoms.

Technical readings use adjusted daily prices and are lagging indicators. This tool does not place orders or forecast a guaranteed top.
