TradingView-style research workspace · isolated from live trading

BacktestBar ReplayData is stored in this browser (IndexedDB) and exportable as CSV

1 · Historical data

1m: max 8 days; 5m+: max 31 days. Use completed UTC dates (default ends yesterday). Data is saved in this browser.
Default: GLD 5-minute bars. Alpaca is attempted first; provider limits determine available history. Imported CSVs are kept locally in this browser.

2 · Strategy & execution settings

Waiting for a dataset.
No-lookahead rule: swing pivots are not available until the configured right-side confirmation bars have closed. A signal appears only after price revisits the 0.618–0.786 pocket and confirms rejection. The backtest engine fills at the next bar open.

3 · Bar replay

No dataset loaded
The chart reveals only candles up to the replay cursor. Use step controls or the slider to inspect how the setup develops. The Golden Box, A/B/C swing levels, entry marks, stop and target are drawn from information available at the selected bar.
